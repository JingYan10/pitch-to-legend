Pitch to Legend – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 November 2026

Pitch to Legend ("the game", "we") is a single-player football career simulator for Android. This policy explains what information the game and the services built into it collect, why, and the choices you have.

The short version

  • There is no account or sign-in. We never ask for your name, email, phone number or contacts.
  • Your career save is stored only on your device. We do not have a server that receives it.
  • The free version shows ads from Google AdMob. AdMob collects some device data to show and measure ads.
  • The optional Pro upgrade is bought through Google Play. Google handles the payment; we never see your card details.

Information stored on your device

Your saved careers, settings and Pro status are kept in the game's local storage on your phone. This data never leaves your device through us. Uninstalling the game, or clearing its storage, deletes it permanently.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The free version uses Google AdMob to show ads, including optional rewarded ads you choose to watch. To serve, measure and protect ads, AdMob may collect and process:

DataPurpose
Advertising ID and other device identifiersAdvertising, analytics, fraud prevention
Approximate location (from your IP address)Advertising, analytics
App interactions (for example, ads viewed or tapped)Advertising, analytics
Crash logs, diagnostics and performance dataAnalytics

This data is sent to Google over an encrypted connection and handled under Google's own policies: Google Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from apps that use its services.

Where the law requires it (for example in the EEA and UK), the game asks for your consent before personalised ads are shown. You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads, in your phone's settings under Google → Ads (or Privacy → Ads).

In-app purchases (Google Play)

The Pro upgrade is sold through Google Play Billing. Google processes the payment and tells the game whether you own Pro, so the purchase can be restored. We receive no payment card or billing address details. Google's handling of purchases is covered by the Google Privacy Policy.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided information through the game, contact us and we will help remove it.

Sharing and selling

We do not sell your personal information. Apart from the data described above going to Google for ads and purchases, we do not share information with anyone.

Your choices and data deletion

  • Delete all game data by clearing the game's storage or uninstalling it.
  • Control ad personalisation and your advertising ID in your device settings.
  • Buying Pro removes ads, which stops the ad-related data collection described above.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
